Case study / Service design

M&T Bank commercial banking transformation

Give a complex service a shared operating language.

A service design program that mapped lending complexity and aligned stakeholders around a clearer future state.

Fragmented processes and repeated handoffs created friction and waste. Van turned research and journey maps into a service blueprint for commercial lending.

Project year 2019 6 min read Updated 2026-05-01

Video summary: The video introduces the commercial lending work and the service blueprint used to connect customer, employee, and operational views.

The story

M&T Bank’s commercial lending experience spanned customers, employees, channels, documents, handoffs, and backstage operations. Friction followed the journey from planning through loan modification.

The core problem was a fragmented service model that obscured the full journey and made improvements difficult to prioritize.
