Vanguard Innovation Lab Integration

A structured critique system that helped innovation teams share learning earlier, pressure-test ideas, and improve decision quality.

The Conflict

Vanguard had innovation talent, strong research instincts, and emerging concepts, but the work was often happening in pockets. Innovation teams were generating ideas, prototypes, and insights without a consistent system for sharing what they were learning across teams.

The risk was that valuable knowledge stayed trapped inside individual projects, while other teams repeated similar discovery work or refined solutions too late in the process.

The Strategic Action

Van recognized that the issue was cultural and operational. The organization did not simply need better presentation polish. It needed a better feedback mechanism. He introduced a structured Design Critique system that gave teams a shared forum to examine concepts while they were still forming.

The critique model shifted the conversation from subjective preference to decision quality. Teams were encouraged to ask sharper questions: What user problem is this solving? What evidence supports this direction? What assumptions still need to be tested? What trade-offs are we making? What should we learn before investing further?

Impact Beyond the Critique System

  • Customer Win: Helped teams pressure-test ideas earlier, before they hardened into expensive solutions. This increased the likelihood that concepts were grounded in real user needs rather than internal assumptions.
  • Organizational Win: Reduced innovation isolation by creating a recurring structure for cross-team learning, peer review, and shared design judgment.
  • Strategic Win: Repositioned critique as a tool for better investment decisions, not just visual refinement. The system helped teams move from “polishing outputs” to improving the quality of the thinking behind those outputs.
  • Execution Win: Accelerated iteration cycles by moving feedback upstream into the concept stage, where teams could still adapt quickly and cheaply.

Strategic Takeaway

Van helped transform design critique from a late-stage review ritual into an early-stage strategic operating system. The work strengthened decision-making, reduced duplication, and helped innovation teams learn from one another while ideas were still flexible.
