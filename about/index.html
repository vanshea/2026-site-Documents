About

The bridge between vision and delivery.

I'm Van, a fractional design leader working across banking, emerging technology, and education. My practice is Human Agency Design.

Art and craft work

Who I work with

A new executive 6 to 18 months into a mandate for change.

A social-impact leader whose funding is in place and whose services now have to catch up to the mission.

A VP or Chief Product Officer with a clear direction and a design org that can't yet translate it into decisions.

The ground has to be fertile. I don't sell the vision on arrival, I make it executable.

How I work: guide and solve

We set the approach together, then I stay embedded long enough to prove it holds. The work is done when the team can carry it forward.

  • Service design
  • AI in the loop
  • Making the invisible visible
  • Strategy to governance

Engagement

Three to six months, two to three days a week, with success measures agreed in week one.
