Section heading

The direction is set. The path to it isn’t.

Use a label, heading, and optional supporting text.

How might your leadership team turn a shared ambition into decisions people can act on — without losing the people doing the work?

This is the question behind every engagement: seeing the whole system, choosing what changes first, and building the capability to keep going.

Drawn from service design work at M&T Bank, Vanguard, Capital One and ConsenSys.

  1. Make the invisible visible.

    Bring customer experience, employee work, policy, and technology into one view. Find the handoffs and decisions that need to change.

  2. Give strategy a working language.

    Use blueprints, prototypes, and clear narratives to make tradeoffs tangible and help leadership and delivery move together.

  3. Put AI in service of judgment.

    Explore where synthesis, simulation, and decision support can help, while keeping responsibility and human agency explicit.

  4. Leave the team more capable.

    Embed critique, decision rules, and working rhythms so that progress continues beyond the engagement.
